Official-source monitoring + discovery leads. The script checks statewide listings plus rotating batches of WA and OR city, county, port and transit agency websites. The agency directory is broader than the websites successfully checked; check the coverage status below. Dates are labeled verified only when visible on a fetched notice. Only design, engineering, planning, signal/ITS and other consulting scopes are included; construction-only bids and construction-management-only contracts are excluded. Compare the issuing agency's original RFP and addenda before submitting a proposal.
